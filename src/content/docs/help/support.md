Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Get in touch.

- **Discord:** [discord.gg/FEvBNh3Xd9](https://discord.gg/FEvBNh3Xd9). This is the fastest way to get help, and you can see what others are asking.
- **Email:** [biomedriveltd@gmail.com](mailto:biomedriveltd@gmail.com)

## When reporting a problem

Include:

1. Your **Unreal Engine version** (5.6, 5.7 or 5.8).
2. The **theme** and **routing style** you were using.
3. What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
4. A **screenshot** or short video, if it's visual.
5. Any lines mentioning **NodeCraft** from the **Output Log** (**Window → Output Log**).

Before reporting, the [FAQ & troubleshooting](/help/faq/) page may already have the answer.
